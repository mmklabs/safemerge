About SafeMerge

SafeMerge is a free set of PDF tools that run entirely in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server.

The short facts

  • What it does: merge, split, rotate, reorder and delete pages, convert images to PDF and PDF to images, add page numbers, and add watermarks.
  • Where files go: nowhere. They are read and changed on your own device and saved back to it.
  • Cost: free. No sign-up, no watermarks on your files, no daily limits.
  • Offline: after the first visit, every tool works without an internet connection.
  • Code: publicly viewable at github.com/mmklabs/safemerge.
  • Made by: mmklabs, an independent developer.

Tools

  • Merge PDF: Combine several PDFs into one, in any order.
  • Compress PDF: Shrink a PDF to 100 KB, 200 KB or any size.
  • Resize image: Photos and signatures to exact pixels and KB.
  • Split PDF: Pull out pages or break a PDF into parts.
  • Rotate PDF: Turn sideways or upside-down pages.
  • Organize PDF: Reorder, delete and rotate pages.
  • JPG to PDF: Turn photos and scans into one PDF.
  • PDF to JPG: Save every page as an image.
  • Page numbers: Number pages in the corner you choose.
  • Watermark PDF: Stamp text like DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL.

How it works

Most online PDF services upload your file to their servers, process it there, and send back the result. SafeMerge does the opposite: the website sends your browser the tools, and your browser does the work. It uses three well-known open-source libraries: pdf-lib for editing PDFs, Mozilla's PDF.js for drawing pages, and JSZip for packing several files into one download.

Check it yourself

  1. Open any tool, then turn off Wi-Fi. The tool keeps working.
  2. Or open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and use a tool. No request carries your file.
  3. Or read the code on GitHub.

Who it's for

Anyone handling documents they'd rather not hand to a stranger's server: bank statements, ID documents, contracts, medical records, payslips, and school or job applications.
