Privacy

Short version: your files never leave your device. We count visits without cookies, and Google ads load only if you allow them.

Your files

Every SafeMerge tool runs inside your web browser. When you choose a file, your browser reads it from your device, changes it there, and saves the result back to your device. Files are never sent to SafeMerge or anyone else. We have no server that could receive them.

You can check this yourself: load a tool, turn off your internet connection, and use it. It still works.

What we collect

We count visits so we know how many people use SafeMerge and which tools are popular. For this we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track you as an individual or across other websites. It records things like the page visited, the referring site, country, browser and device type, and page load speed. It never sees your files.

There are no accounts, no advertising trackers and no SafeMerge cookies. We remember one setting in your browser's local storage, your ad cookie choice, so we don't ask again. It stays on your device.

Hosting

Like every website, our hosting provider handles the requests that load these pages and may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time) for security. Those requests never contain your files.

Your rights

Because SafeMerge stores no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, use My Ad Center. California residents: SafeMerge does not sell personal information. Choosing "Cookie-free ads only" opts you out of Google ad cookies on this site.
